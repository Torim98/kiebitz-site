02Services

Kiebitz is a local-first chess companion for desktop and Android. Analysis, training and data storage run on your devices; the provider operates no server that receives your games.

Kiebitz Plus unlocks the features listed in the overview on the Kiebitz Plus page and removes the ads. Unlocking works through a signed entitlement that your device verifies locally; one account covers desktop and Android together and is meant for your own devices.

Specific analysis results, a specific gain in playing strength, or the availability of third-party services such as chess.com, Lichess, chessdb.cn, Stripe or Google Play are not owed. Kiebitz Free stays fully usable in any case.

03Account and sign-in

Sign-in works without a password: you receive a one-time link by email that is valid for 15 minutes and can be used only once. Keep your mailbox secure and do not pass sign-in links on.

You can only enter into a Kiebitz Plus contract if you have full legal capacity. The account is personal; passing it to third parties is not permitted. You can delete your account at any time on the account page or in the app; a running subscription must be cancelled first.

04Conclusion of contract

The presentation of Kiebitz Plus on this website and in the app is not a binding offer but an invitation to order. You make a binding offer by completing the order in the checkout; the contract is concluded as soon as the provider confirms or unlocks the subscription.

On this website checkout runs through Stripe, in the Android app through Google Play. The respective provider’s terms additionally apply to processing the purchase. Before you complete the order, the service, term and price are shown again; you can correct input errors there until you submit.

The contract text is not stored separately. These terms are permanently available at this address and can be printed or saved; your order data is available at Stripe or in your Google account.

05Prices and payment

Kiebitz Plus currently costs €1.99 per month. The price is a final price and includes statutory VAT where it applies. The price shown to you in the checkout always prevails.

  • Billing is monthly in advance via the payment method you selected.
  • Payment details stay with Stripe or Google, not with Kiebitz; those providers issue the receipts.
  • If a payment fails, access to Plus can be suspended after prior notice or the contract terminated. Kiebitz Free stays usable.
  • Price changes are announced by email at least 30 days in advance and apply from the following billing period; you can cancel until then.

06Free trial

Kiebitz Plus can be tried free for 7 days. The trial is available once per person; deleting and re-creating an account does not produce a second trial. A valid payment method is required to start, but nothing is charged during the trial.

If you do not cancel before the trial ends, it automatically turns into the paid subscription and the first payment falls due. You can see the end date at any time on your account page.

07Term and cancellation

The subscription runs for one month and renews automatically by one further month unless you cancel. You can cancel at any time with effect from the end of the current billing period — no notice period, no reason required.

Subscriptions bought through Stripe are cancelled on the account page under “Manage subscription”, those bought through Google Play in the Play Store. After cancellation Kiebitz Plus stays active until the end of the paid period and then falls back to Kiebitz Free automatically. Your local games, analyses and training data remain unchanged on your devices. The right to terminate for good cause remains unaffected for both sides.

08Right of withdrawal for consumers

Consumers have the right to withdraw from the contract within 14 days of its conclusion without giving reasons. An unambiguous statement is enough — for example an email to support@kiebitz.dev or a letter to the address above. Sending the statement before the deadline expires is sufficient to meet it.

Early expiry: Kiebitz Plus is unlocked immediately. The right of withdrawal therefore expires once the provider has begun performance after you expressly agreed in the checkout that performance should begin before the withdrawal period ends and confirmed that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal. If you withdraw beforehand, the provider refunds all payments received within 14 days using the same payment method; proportionate compensation may be due for the service already provided.
